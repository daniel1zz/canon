---
id: BR-2009
title: Changed rule and decision text is reviewed against its whole domain before landing
status: active
---
When a landing changes a file under `lore/rules` or `lore/decisions` and a reviewer is configured, the reviewer receives the new text plus the whole current context of each touched domain. A `block` verdict refuses the landing and main does not move; a reviewer that fails to run or prints no valid verdict also refuses it.
