---
id: BR-2014
title: Main moves only after the floor passes on the exact tree that becomes main
status: active
---
The floor command runs in the worktree after it is rebased onto main, i.e. on the tree that becomes main. A non-zero exit refuses the landing (exit 1), main does not move, and the full output is kept in a log named in the refusal. A project without a floor cannot land anything.
